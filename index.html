  5. 05Agreed follow-upUntil the vessel is ready
Scope note

A private pre-inspection is preventive. It does not equal or replace a statutory survey, Flag State approval, Class certification or official endorsement. Those actions belong to duly authorized authorities and organizations.

Pillar III · Training

Professional Crew Development

Practical training on board, where competence is actually used.

  • Vessel familiarization
  • Safety procedures & drills
  • Crew preparation for inspections
  • Operational exercises
  • Competence development
Training scope

Practical training is non-certifying. Official STCW courses and certificates are offered only under the corresponding approvals and requirements. IMO instructor experience does not by itself confer institutional authorization to issue STCW certificates.
